How did the child care subsidy applicable percentage for the lowest income band change between the 2020 and 2026 compilations?
Answer

The percentage rose from 85% to 90%.1 In the compilation in force from 1 July 2020 it was 85% at or below the lower income threshold of $65,710;2 from 1 July 2026 it is 90%, with a base threshold of $80,000 indexed annually.3

Sch 2 cl 3(1)

Table item 1: an individual whose adjusted taxable income does not exceed the lower income threshold has an applicable percentage of 90%.

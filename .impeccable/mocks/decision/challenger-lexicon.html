How did the child care subsidy applicable percentage for the lowest income band change between the 2020 and 2026 compilations?

Answer.

It rose by five percentage points: from 85 per cent in the compilation in force from 1 July 2020 to 90 per cent in the compilation in force from 1 July 2026 (Family Assistance Act 1999, Sch. 2, cl. 3(1), item 1).

The lower income threshold was $65,710 in 2020; the 2026 compilation states a base of $80,000, indexed annually.

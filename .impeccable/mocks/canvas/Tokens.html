Cue lights and state

State always pairs a light with an icon and a word. Green is go and done, amber is standby or degraded, red is failed or a regression. Pass and fail never rely on colour alone. Words: the chat says Running, Done, Failed; the dashboard says Pass, Fail, Regression.

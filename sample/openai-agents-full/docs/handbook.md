# Acme Logistics Operations Handbook

## 1. Definitions

**Cold chain** means the temperature-controlled path a perishable SKU follows from inbound dock to outbound carrier.

**Reservation** means a customer-specific hold on stock that reduces available quantity without shipping it.

## 2. Cold-chain handling

### 2.1 Temperature range

Cold-chain goods must stay between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius at all times. A reading outside this range for more than 15 minutes triggers a quarantine per section 4.

### 2.2 Dispatch window

Cold-chain SKUs are dispatched Monday to Wednesday only (see the shipping policy).

## 3. Hazardous goods labelling

Every hazardous carton carries a GHS pictogram, the UN number, and the emergency contact card. Bay H is the only permitted storage location, with 3 metres of clearance from ignition sources.

## 4. Quarantine

Quarantined stock is moved to bay Q, tagged with the incident id, and released only after a supervisor signs the temperature log.
